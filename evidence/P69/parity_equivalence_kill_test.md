# P69 — Parity-equivalence kill test (2026-10-28)

## Exact algebraic identity
For positive-definite symmetric G and antisymmetric B, define E_{G,B}(m,n)=(m+Bn)^T G^{-1}(m+Bn)+n^T G n and s(m,n)=m.n. Under (G,B;m,n)->(G,-B;m,-n), m+(-B)(-n)=m+Bn and n^T G n is unchanged. Thus E is exactly preserved while s changes sign. The integral charge map T=diag(I,-I) obeys T^T J T=-J, hence it is an anti-isometry of the Lorentzian form, not an O(d,d;Z) isometry. Therefore a spin-refined difference for B versus -B cannot alone establish inequivalence of unoriented CFTs: worldsheet parity is a potential explanation. Nor does it establish equality as *oriented* CFTs without specifying the allowed equivalences. This is a kill test of the naive novelty claim, not a proof that all physically normalized genus-two amplitudes coincide.

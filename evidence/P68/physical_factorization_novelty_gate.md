# P68 — physical factorization consequence and novelty kill-test

Date: 2026-10-28. Status: research gate, no manuscript.

## Precisely delimited consequence
For a Narain charge lattice (Z^(2d),J,Q), a tensor factorization into two *toroidal current-algebra-preserving* sectors requires an integral direct sum L1 ⊕ L2 that is orthogonal under both J and Q (after an integral change of charge basis). Thus P65/P66 non-splitting implies no such factorization. This is a necessary-condition obstruction, not a classification of arbitrary abstract CFT tensor products. In particular it does not imply a new genus-two discrepancy or any physical partition-function normalization.

## Verification and limitations
Algebraic proof: in a product of independent toroidal sectors, momentum-winding charges split integrally; left and right quadratic forms are block diagonal. Their sum Q and difference J are consequently block orthogonal. Contrapositive proves the stated obstruction. No converse is claimed for an arbitrary abstract CFT factorization. No new numerical experiments are required for this logical consequence; no normalized genus-two calculation was performed.

Major novelty kill: Dymarsky/Shapere already give physically distinct isospectral Narain/code CFTs; Henriksson/Kakkar/McPeak show higher-genus distinctions. Thus generic spectral non-uniqueness and higher-genus resolution are not original claims. The present B-sign parity relation is elementary and weaker than a physically distinct isospectral construction.

The P17 local independent audit executed with exit code 0 and reproduced the P13 T3/T4 characteristic polynomials and P15/P16 T6 exact threshold, 289 states and signed coefficients. This is a distinct integer-scaled one-sign implementation; it is NOT independent proof of the P13 complete O(d,d;Z) claim, nor a verbatim P11 audit.

Verdict: NO-GO for the proposed broad Q1-level manuscript; REVISE for a tightly scoped exact-certificate note only after P11 and novelty tests.
